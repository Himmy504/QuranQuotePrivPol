Privacy Policy

App: QuranQuote
Package: com.quranquote.app
Effective date: May 4, 2026

Summary

QuranQuote is a Quran quote/verse image editor. The app is designed to work primarily on-device. We do not sell your personal data.

This policy describes how the app handles information when you use features like selecting a background image, exporting images to your gallery, and sharing a generated quote image.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or address.

The app may process the following information on your device:

  • Selected content (e.g., surah/ayah selection, translation choice, and styling settings) to render your quote.
  • Images you choose (optional) if you pick a custom background from your device.
  • Generated images that you export to your device gallery or share via Android’s share sheet.
  • App local storage (e.g., cached fonts and local translation data) used for offline rendering and performance.

How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Render Quran text and translations in the editor preview.
  • Apply your design settings (fonts, colors, spacing, backgrounds, effects).
  • Export the final image to your gallery and/or prepare it for sharing.
  • Download or cache required Quran fonts for proper rendering (when needed).

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

The app shares content only when you explicitly choose to share an exported image using the Android share sheet. In that case, the receiving app/service (for example, a messaging or social media app) will process the shared image under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Depending on your Android version and your actions, QuranQuote may request access to:

  • Photos/Media: to let you pick a custom background image.
  • Storage / Media access (system-managed): to save exported images to your gallery.

The app’s editor works without selecting a background image. Media access is only needed for features you initiate.

Third-Party Services

QuranQuote may use third-party libraries to function (for example, image decoding/loading). These libraries operate within the app and are used to deliver app functionality.

Data Retention

Your editor settings, cached assets (such as fonts), and translation data may be stored locally on your device to improve performance and support offline use. You can remove this data by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings.

Exported images are stored in your device gallery and remain there until you delete them.

Children’s Privacy

QuranQuote is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when we do.
